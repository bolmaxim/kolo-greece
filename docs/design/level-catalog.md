# MVP Level Catalog

The MVP contains 22 short levels: seven in Meteora, seven in Corfu, and eight in Santorini.

## Meteora

1. **First Roll** — movement and stopping on narrow ledges.
2. **Sesame Trail** — jumping and optional collectible route.
3. **Weight of Water** — first Heavy transformation.
4. **Bronze Plate** — Heavy Kolo activates a pressure plate.
5. **Rope Above the Valley** — plate and hanging platform sequence.
6. **Cracked Bridge** — Heavy Kolo breaks weak flooring.
7. **Monastery Ascent** — combined puzzle followed by a short chase.

## Corfu

1. **Old Port** — buoyancy and shallow water.
2. **Olive Grove** — rolling speed on natural slopes.
3. **Honey Wall** — first Sticky transformation.
4. **Canal Crossing** — moving boats and water gaps.
5. **Wind in the Sails** — redirecting wind into a sail.
6. **Fortress Drain** — changing the water level.
7. **Storm over Kerkyra** — combined Sticky puzzle and chase.

## Santorini

1. **White Roofs** — readable routes across layered roofs.
2. **Blue Dome** — vertical jumps and curved surfaces.
3. **Baker's Oven** — first Solid transformation.
4. **Mill Blades** — timing around rotating mechanisms.
5. **Caldera Wind** — controlled air currents.
6. **Roof Collapse** — Solid impacts and falling routes.
7. **Sunset Run** — state combination and chase.
8. **The Great Mill** — puzzle boss combining all learned rules.

Each level has three optional goals: all sesame seeds, no damage, and one secret or secondary physical challenge.
